About

A small, open reference for setting up LS-DYNA material models without retyping the same cards from the manual.

What is here

  • Library: the list of supported models with short theory notes.
  • Generator: each model has default inputs. The generator derives the remaining parameters and writes a fixed-format keyword card.
  • Samples: keyword decks you can download and run as-is, for some models.

Conventions

All generators use the mm · ms · g · N · MPa unit system. Density is in g/mm³ and time is in ms.

Disclaimer

Generated cards are a starting point. Check them against the LS-DYNA Keyword User's Manual for your solver version, and calibrate them against test data before use in design or research. LS-DYNA is a trademark of Ansys, Inc. This site is not affiliated with Ansys or LST.

Who maintains it

The site is maintained by a postdoctoral researcher at INSA Rennes (France) working on LS-DYNA modelling of concrete and steel structures. Generators are checked against the LS-DYNA R13 Keyword User's Manual. Where noted on a model page, they are also checked with single-element tests.
